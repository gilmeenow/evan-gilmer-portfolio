Context

SBIRT (Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment) is a statewide behavioral-health service model administered under a New York State cooperative agreement with OASAS. The program relied on a companion mHealth app for providers and patients, but adoption and follow-through were inconsistent across sites.

Role & Approach

I managed the initiative's $10M SAMHSA-funded delivery — vendor coordination, compliance, and stakeholder deliverables — while personally leading the UX research effort: a mixed-methods study combining group interviews, contextual inquiry, diary studies, and surveys to find where the app was losing users.

What I Did

  • Designed and ran a mixed-methods UX study of the mobile health app, identifying specific friction points in the screening and referral flow.
  • Led A/B testing on app changes and iterated the product design based on results.
  • Developed the Treatment Progress Assessment (TPA-8) to evaluate patient outcomes across state-funded outpatient treatment providers.
  • Worked with the OASAS Associate Commissioner's office to build web-based distance-learning training modules reflecting mandated standards of care.
  • Oversaw vendor coordination, compliance requirements, timelines, and stakeholder deliverables for the full cooperative agreement.

Context

Kidney care is one of the clearest test cases for value-based payment (VBP) in the U.S. health system, and Northwell Health was evaluating a direct-to-consumer telehealth platform to support it. Before scaling, the partnership needed real evidence of provider readiness — not just executive sponsorship.

Role & Approach

I designed and facilitated primary research directly with the providers who would need to adopt the platform, then translated what they told us into recommendations Northwell's health-system partners could act on.

What I Did

  • Facilitated 5 regional focus groups with 75+ providers to assess real-world market readiness for the platform.
  • Collected and synthesized qualitative provider feedback alongside value-based-payment adoption data.
  • Translated findings into strategic recommendations for Northwell Health and its health-system partners.
  • Coordinated the research effort as part of the broader $10M digital health initiative's stakeholder deliverables.
